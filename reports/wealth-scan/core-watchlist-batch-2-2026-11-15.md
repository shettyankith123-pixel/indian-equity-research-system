### Infosys (INFY) — WAIT; IT exposure already high in the user's portfolio
- Close ₹1,141.50; trailing P/E about 15.1–15.7x; P/B about 4.7–5.0x; 52-week range ₹980.40–₹1,728.
- Q2 FY27 revenue US$5.145bn, +1.5% YoY and +1.2% QoQ constant currency; operating margin 21.0%; net profit ₹7,823cr (+6.2%); large-deal TCV US$5.2bn with 62% net new; AI revenue 10.5% of revenue and +29% QoQ; free cash flow ₹8,898cr.
- FY27 constant-currency revenue guidance was cut to 1–2%, while margin guidance stayed at 20–22%. This is the key counterpoint to strong deal wins and AI revenue. Demand, pricing and conversion of signed deals remain uncertain.
- **Action:** WAIT for a clearer demand/guidance inflection. Valuation is lower than many premium large-cap names, but the user's current portfolio is already nearly 60% TCS; adding another IT services name would worsen sector concentration.
- Watch: deal conversion, North America demand, AI monetisation, revenue guidance, attrition and margins.
- INDmoney analyst mean target ₹1,160 (+1.6%) is not intrinsic fair value.

### Bajaj Finance (BAJFINANCE) — HOLD / WAIT FOR BETTER VALUATION
- Close ₹1,117; trailing P/E about 36.6x; P/B about 6.1x; 52-week range ₹787.90–₹1,176.40.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated net total income ₹15,974cr (+23% YoY), PAT ₹6,367cr (+29%), AUM ₹5,84,624cr (+26.5%), NII +24%. GNPA 1.06%, NNPA 0.42%; capital adequacy 20.33%; leverage 5.1x. Loan losses/provisions ₹2,166cr, up 4%, including ₹337cr prudent macro provision. Cost of funds 7.31%.
- Strong growth, distribution, risk analytics and profitability are positives. However, high valuation, unsecured/consumer credit cycle, overdue/Stage-2 migration, funding costs and capital raise/dilution need attention. The announced QIP/warrant capital raise should be evaluated for dilution versus incremental ROE.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT. The franchise is high quality, but ~36.6x earnings leaves a demanding entry valuation. Reassess after credit-cost trends and capital raise terms are clear.
- Watch: Stage-2 assets, GNPA/NNPA, credit cost, NIM, funding mix, return on assets/equity and diluted share count.
- Analyst mean target ₹1,200 (+7.4%) is sentiment only.
- Sources: Bajaj Finance Q2 FY27 investor presentation filing https://indianstockalerts.com/news/bajfinance-investor-presentation-36824 ; Bajaj Finserv press release https://www.aboutbajajfinserv.com/bfsl/press-release/fy-2026-27/Q2_Press_Release ; valuation snapshot https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/bajaj-finance-ltd/657

### Larsen & Toubro (LT) — HOLD / ACCUMULATE ONLY ON CORRECTIONS
- Close ₹4,143.80; P/E about 31.9–36.3x depending on EPS source; P/B about 5.0–5.2x; 52-week range ₹3,288.10–₹4,440.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹78,750cr (+16% YoY), PAT ₹5,202cr (+33%), and record quarterly order inflows ₹1,47,665cr (+28%). International revenue was 58% of total. The H2 opportunity pipeline was reported at about ₹10.3 lakh crore.
- A diversified engineering/EPC franchise with strong execution history and broad infrastructure, energy and manufacturing exposure. Counterpoints are high valuation, project-margin execution, working-capital intensity, overseas geopolitical/contract risk and order-book conversion. INDmoney news feed reported a Karnataka blacklisting order over delayed Jal Jeevan Mission work; primary government order, scope and any appeal/contract impact must be verified before assigning a governance or financial penalty.
- **Action:** HOLD; only ACCUMULATE on meaningful corrections or when order conversion and cash generation support the premium. Current price is close to its 52-week high and analyst target upside is modest.
- Watch: order inflows versus revenue, execution margins, working capital, international project risk, cash conversion and the Karnataka order's verified legal status.
- Analyst mean target ₹4,466.50 (+7.8%) is sentiment only.
- Sources: Q2 FY27 results release https://financialfilings.com/filings/larsen-toubro-limited/earnings-release/2026/74788145/ ; earnings call transcript https://investors.larsentoubro.com/upload/Analysttrans/FY2027AnalysttransL%26T%20Q2FY27%20Earnings%20Call%20Transcript.pdf ; valuation snapshot https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/43056/larsen-toubro-ltd/

### NTPC — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY
- Close ₹319.10; trailing P/E about 11.4x; P/B about 1.5x; dividend yield about 2.8%; 52-week range ₹292.05–₹414.40.
- Q2 FY27 standalone total income ₹46,842cr (+15.1%) and PAT ₹5,320cr (+14%). Consolidated group PAT ₹7,351cr (+40.7%). H1 standalone PAT +13.1%; power generation and operational efficiency supported results. NTPC is pursuing expansion toward a 149 GW capacity ambition by 2032 and diversifying into renewables, storage and nuclear feasibility.
- Regulated generation scale and long-term power demand support the thesis. Risks include coal availability/price, plant availability, receivables, capex funding/debt, regulatory returns, renewable execution and transition risk. Consolidated profit growth is materially stronger than standalone growth, so subsidiary/associate contribution should be understood.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY in small tranches; low P/E and a diversified power-generation role are attractive, but leverage and capital intensity mean this is not a low-risk bond substitute.
- Watch: plant load factor, generation growth, coal stocks, receivables, net debt, regulated equity returns, renewable commissioning and capex.
- Analyst mean target ₹431.50 (+35.2%) is sentiment only.
- Sources: NTPC Q2 FY27 press release https://ntpc.co.in/media/press-releases/ntpc-announces-robust-financial-performance-h1-fy27 ; Q2/H1 financial results https://ntpc.co.in/sites/default/files/financial_results/2026/QFRQ2FY27%20Final.pdf ; investor presentation https://ntpc.co.in/sites/default/files/conference_call/2026/NTPC_OFS_30.09.2026.pdf ; market metrics https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/ntpc-ltd/82

### Reliance Industries (RELIANCE) — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY; diversified growth at a moderate multiple
- Close ₹1,339.70; P/E about 22.4x; P/B about 2.0x; 52-week range ₹1,152.20–₹1,611.80.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated gross revenue ₹3,62,268cr (+27.8% YoY), EBITDA ₹62,409cr (+23.9%) and PAT ₹29,550cr (+33.8%). O2C EBITDA reached a record ₹25,203cr (+67.9%); digital services EBITDA ₹22,223cr (+17.7%). Overall EBITDA margin was 17.2%, down 60 bps YoY.
- Diversified earnings across O2C, Jio/digital and retail, plus new-energy investments, create several growth levers. However, segment mix is important: O2C may be cyclical, retail margins/profitability need monitoring, and new-energy projects require substantial capital and execution. The Jio Platforms capital-market debut is a potential value-unlock but should not be treated as guaranteed future upside.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY for a long horizon; avoid a large single entry. Current valuation is more moderate than many consumer franchises, but the business is complex and capital-intensive.
- Watch: Jio ARPU/subscriber growth, retail EBITDA and store productivity, O2C margins, net debt, new-energy capex returns and any Jio listing-related capital allocation.
- Analyst mean target ₹1,690 (+26.2%) is sentiment only.
