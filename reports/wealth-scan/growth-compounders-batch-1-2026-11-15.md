## 1) Astral (ASTRAL) — WAIT
- Close ₹1,254; P/E approximately 59.5–62.8x; P/B about 8.0–8.3x; 52-week range ₹1,215.50–₹1,768.70.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹1,988cr (+26%), EBITDA ₹272cr (+1.5%), EBITDA margin 13.7% versus 17.0%, PAT ₹127cr (-5.6%). Plumbing volume grew 19.1%; paints/adhesives revenue +28.6%, but segment EBITDA fell 19.2%.
- Strong plumbing brand/distribution and demand are positives. But margin compression in plumbing and especially paints/adhesives, plus ~60x earnings, makes the entry unattractive without evidence of recovery.
- **Action:** WAIT. Revenue growth alone is not enough when EBITDA is nearly flat and PAT is down.
- Watch: CPVC/PVC spreads, plumbing volume, paints/adhesives margins, working capital, cash conversion and management's Q3 margin-recovery guidance.
- Analyst mean target ₹1,723 is sentiment only.

## 2) Supreme Industries (SUPREMEIND) — ACCUMULATE ON CORRECTIONS
- Close ₹3,307.50; P/E approximately 37.5–41.3x; P/B about 6.6–6.8x; 52-week range ₹3,078.50–₹4,074.80.
- Q2 FY27 standalone plastic goods volume 184,455 MT (+19.4%); total income ₹3,416cr (+39.3%); operating profit ₹431cr (+45.0%); PAT ₹279cr (+44.5%). Consolidated revenue ₹3,361cr, EBITDA ₹461cr and PAT ₹262cr; net cash surplus ₹685cr at 30-Sep-2026.
- Strong piping demand, product breadth and net-cash balance sheet support the thesis. Risks include PVC/resin volatility, construction demand, product mix, capacity utilisation and a reported FY27 volume-growth guidance reduction to around 10% that needs confirmation from the official call transcript.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE ON CORRECTIONS, in tranches. Results show operating leverage and balance-sheet strength, but the P/E is still above a plain industrial multiple.
- Watch: volume growth, EBITDA/tonne, PVC spreads, net cash, capex utilisation and management's revised volume outlook.
- Analyst mean target ₹4,035 is sentiment only.
- Sources: Q2 FY27 result filing https://financialfilings.com/filings/supreme-industries-ltd/earnings-release/2026/74748150/ ; valuation snapshot https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/44696/supreme-industries-ltd/

## 3) KEI Industries (KEI) — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY ON WEAKNESS
- Close ₹4,040; P/E approximately 36.6–42.1x; P/B about 5.4–5.8x; 52-week range ₹3,666.10–₹5,899.
- Q2 FY27 standalone revenue ₹3,776cr (+38.5%), EBITDA ₹415cr (+28.7%), PAT ₹262cr (+29%). Domestic wires and cables sales grew 45.8%; exports +7.3%. H1 revenue +30.9%, EBITDA +34.0%, PAT +34.4%.
- Strong structural demand from power, housing, infrastructure and industrial electrification. Risks include copper/aluminium volatility, margin pressure, project execution, customer/channel concentration and elevated valuation despite the recent correction.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY on weakness, not a full-size entry at once. The stock has corrected materially from its 52-week high and results remain strong, but the valuation still requires sustained growth.
- Watch: domestic vs export growth, EBITDA margin, capacity utilisation, copper spreads, working capital and promoter/FII holding changes.
- Analyst mean target ₹5,821 is sentiment only.

## 4) HDFC Asset Management Company (HDFCAMC) — HOLD / WAIT
- Close ₹2,663; P/E about 38.4x; P/B about 13.3x; 52-week range ₹2,205.60–₹2,901.50.
- Q2 FY27 quarterly average AUM ₹9.69 lakh crore (+10% YoY); active equity AUM ₹6.08 lakh crore (+14%) with 12.7% market share. Revenue from operations ₹1,169cr (+14%), operating profit ₹907cr (+16%), PBT ₹978cr (+12%), PAT ₹750cr. Individual investors contributed 70% of monthly average AUM; live accounts 32.3m.
- Asset-light economics, brand, distribution and high operating margins are positives. Risks include market-level AUM volatility, fee compression, passive/index competition, market-share changes and high P/B/P/E.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT. Business quality is high, but valuation is not cheap enough to justify chasing; buy only with a stronger margin of safety.
- Watch: active equity market share, net flows, SIP growth, fee yields, expense ratio, passive products and operating margin.
- Analyst mean target ₹3,100 is sentiment only.

## 8) Radico Khaitan (RADICO) — WAIT; premiumisation is real, valuation is very high
- Close ₹4,605; P/E approximately 77–102x across providers; P/B about 16.8–18.6x; 52-week range ₹2,500–₹4,747.
- Q2 FY27 revenue ₹1,745cr (+16.8%), EBITDA ₹356.5cr (+51%), EBITDA margin 20.5% versus 15.8%; IMFL volumes +7.1%, while Prestige & Above volumes +35.7% and revenue +38.6%. Premium products contributed 53.7% of IMFL volumes and 77.1% of IMFL sales value.
- Premiumisation and brands such as Rampur and Magic Moments are attractive. Risks include very high valuation, excise/tariff regulation, state-level distribution policy, input/packaging costs, premium demand cyclicality and concentration in a few brands. A reported customs seizure is small relative to the business but should be reconciled with the company's filing.
- **Action:** WAIT. Excellent operating momentum is already priced in at 77–102x earnings; avoid paying any price for a premiumisation story.
- Watch: P&A mix, gross/EBITDA margin, state policy, excise duty, Scotch import tariff, net debt and cash conversion.
- Analyst mean target ₹4,750 is only 3.2% above the close and is not intrinsic value.
- Sources: Q2 FY27 result analysis https://www.icicidirect.com/research/equity/rapid-results/radico-khaitan-ltd ; earnings analysis https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_radico_q2fy27.pdf ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/43936/radico-khaitan-ltd/
