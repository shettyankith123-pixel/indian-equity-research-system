### Marico (MARICO) — WAIT; strong operating momentum, expensive entry
- Close ₹900; P/E approximately 56.3x; 52-week range ₹706.75–₹925.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹3,897cr (+13% YoY), EBITDA ₹709cr (+27%), EBITDA margin 18.2% (+200 bps), PAT ₹550cr (+31%); domestic volume growth 10%, international constant-currency growth 13%. H1 revenue +18%, EBITDA +26%, PAT +28%.
- Parachute/Saffola brand equity, pricing, distribution, premiumisation and improving margins support the thesis. Risks include high P/E, edible-oil input volatility, rural demand and execution/integration of new wellness acquisitions.
- **Action:** WAIT. Business momentum is good, but ~56x earnings and a share price close to its 52-week high leave limited margin of safety. Revisit on a meaningful correction or if earnings compound fast enough to reduce the multiple.
- Watch: volume growth, gross margin, copra/edible-oil costs, international growth, new-brand contribution and acquisition returns.
- Analyst mean target ₹965 (+7.2%) is sentiment only.

### Hindustan Unilever (HINDUNILVR) — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY ON CORRECTIONS
- Close ₹1,992.90; P/E approximately 31.0x; P/B approximately 9.5x; dividend yield about 2.1%; 52-week range ₹1,758.20–₹2,488.
- Q2 FY27 turnover ₹18,071cr; underlying sales growth 15%, underlying volume growth 7%; EBITDA ₹4,089cr (+8%), margin 22.6%; PAT before exceptional items +9%. Reported PAT ₹2,796cr was flat, with prior-year base affected by one-off tax-related gain. Interim dividend ₹19/share.
- Broad household/personal care brands, distribution and high ROE support durability. Recent volume growth suggests consumption momentum improving. Risks include input-cost inflation, price increases that may affect volumes, competition from regional/value brands, premium valuation and food-claim/regulatory notices that need primary review.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY on corrections, not as a large one-time buy. Current P/E is lower than some historical periods but remains a premium multiple; require sustained volume-led growth and margin stability.
- Watch: volume growth, price/mix, ad spend, EBITDA margin, rural recovery, premiumisation, regulatory notices and market-share trends.
- Analyst mean target ₹2,445 (+22.7%) is sentiment only.

### ITC — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY; tax shock is the key risk
- Close ₹285; P/E approximately 18.9x; P/B approximately 5.0x; dividend yield about 5.1%; 52-week range ₹252.80–₹412.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue grew 5% overall; non-cigarette revenue +20%; FMCG Others +19%. The company's like-to-like FMCG revenue grew 17.3%, with FMCG EBITDA margin expanding 50 bps. Overall consolidated EBITDA fell 16% YoY as the cigarette business faced an unprecedented tax increase and legal cigarette volumes remained under pressure. Agri business grew 12%; Century Pulp and Paper acquisition was completed on 1-Aug-2026.
- Diversified cash-generating businesses, strong balance sheet and high dividend yield are positives. The cigarette tax change creates risk of volume downtrading to illicit products and could structurally alter the profit pool. Non-cigarette FMCG growth is promising but is not yet large enough to fully offset cigarette weakness.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY in small tranches for a long horizon; do not assume the cigarette earnings shock is temporary. Reassess after the tax impact and legal-market volume trend become clearer.
- Watch: legal cigarette volumes, tax/illicit trade, price elasticity, FMCG segment EBITDA, paperboard margins, agri cycle, hotel subsidiary and dividend coverage.
- Analyst mean target ₹320 (+12.3%) is sentiment only.
- Sources: ITC Q2 FY27 company press release https://itcportal.com/media-centre/press-releases/media-statement-financial-results-for-the-quarter-ended-30th-september-2026.html ; detailed results release https://itcportal.com/content/dam/itc-corporate/pdfs/financial-result/quarterly-results-2026-2027/september-2026/ITC-Press-Release-Q2-FY2027.pdf ; market metrics https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/itc-ltd/1353
