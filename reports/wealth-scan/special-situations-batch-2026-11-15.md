## 1) CDSL — WAIT; governance/technology controls need remediation evidence
- Close ₹1,447; P/E approximately 59.9x; P/B about 15.2x; 52-week range ₹1,116.30–₹1,673.70.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated total income grew 22% YoY and net profit 24%; H1 total income +19% and net profit +20%. CDSL serves more than 19 crore demat accounts.
- Strong structural tailwind from demat penetration and capital-market participation, with high margins and network effects. However, SEBI imposed a ₹1 crore penalty on 30-Oct-2026 for operational/IT lapses from FY2023-24 inspection, including pledge-rejection communication, CUSA processes, vulnerability patching, data-loss prevention and disaster-recovery deficiencies. CDSL disclosed no material financial impact other than the penalty, but the control failures are important for a systemically critical depository.
- **Action:** WAIT. Do not pay ~60x earnings until remediation evidence, independent cyber assurance and governance follow-through are reviewed.
- Watch: SEBI appeal/payment status, closure of each finding, VAPT scope, disaster recovery testing, incident history, demat account growth and transaction revenue.
- Sources: CDSL official Q2 FY27 results https://www.cdslindia.com/downloads/InvestorRels/Financial/Financials_Q2_2027.pdf ; CDSL media release https://www.cdslindia.com/Downloads/About/Media/Media%20Release%20-%20CDSL%20declares%20financial%20results%20for%20Q2%20and%20H1%20FY27_Updated.pdf ; SEBI penalty coverage https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-imposes-1-crore-fine-on-cdsl-for-multiple-compliance-lapses-126103001384_1.html ; market snapshot https://www.plenitude.co.in/pages/cdsl-share-price

## 2) Anand Rathi Wealth (ANANDRATHI) — WAIT; valuation far ahead of growth
- Close ₹2,381.20; P/E approximately 87–100x; P/B approximately 20–31x; 52-week range ₹1,353.85–₹2,410.
- Q2 FY27 AUM reached ₹1,08,377cr (+18.4% YoY). Adjusted consolidated revenue ₹356.6cr (+16.1%), adjusted PAT ₹121.9cr (+22.2%). Reported consolidated PAT was ₹89.3cr versus ₹99.9cr, reflecting non-cash ESOP costs of about ₹46cr; H1 adjusted PAT grew about 23%.
- Wealth-management distribution and recurring client assets support the business, but AUM is market-sensitive, client/RM productivity matters and the stock trades at a very high multiple. Reported versus adjusted earnings need consistent treatment.
- **Action:** WAIT. Even with good AUM growth, ~87–100x earnings offers little margin of safety.
- Watch: net inflows, AUM growth versus market appreciation, RM productivity, client concentration, ESOP dilution/costs, fee yields and cash conversion.
- Sources: Q2 FY27 earnings release https://financialfilings.com/filings/anand-rathi-wealth-limited/earnings-release/2026/68335860/ ; investor presentation https://www.bazaarwatch.com/announcement/205035/anand-rathi-wealth-limited-investor-presentation ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/289782/anand-rathi-wealth-ltd/

## 3) BSE Ltd (BSE) — HOLD / WAIT; market activity is strong, valuation is full
- Close ₹3,937.70; P/E approximately 64x; P/B approximately 24x; 52-week range ₹2,377.40–₹4,446.80.
- Q2 FY27 revenue from operations ₹1,433cr (+35.9% YoY), total income ₹1,551cr, continuing-operations PAT ₹807.5cr (+45%). H1 revenue from operations +48% and continuing PAT +55%; operating EBITDA margin around 67–69%.
- Exchange network effects, derivatives market share, IPO/listing services and clearing income support high profitability. Counterpoints: revenues are sensitive to trading volumes and regulatory changes (especially index derivatives), NSE competition, market-cycle risk, high valuation and CEO/management succession.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT. A strong quarter is not enough to justify ~64x earnings when the price is near its 52-week high and analyst consensus is around the current price.
- Watch: derivatives premium market share, cash-market share, regulatory/STT changes, clearing income, technology uptime, CEO succession and operating costs.
- Sources: BSE Q2 FY27 filing https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/BSE1_04112026180514_NSEINTIMATION.pdf ; earnings summary https://quartr.com/events/bse-limited-bse-q2-26-27_F4AuAiDe ; valuation https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/bse-ltd/78

## 4) MCX — WAIT; excellent operating leverage, expensive entry
- Close ₹3,720; P/E approximately 52.7–71.2x; P/B approximately 19–33x; 52-week range ₹1,875.70–₹3,767.40.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue from operations ₹799.7cr (+113.7% YoY), PAT ₹454.6cr (+130.2%); H1 revenue +100.9% and PAT +116.6%. Commodity turnover and options participation have increased sharply.
- Dominant commodity derivatives platform with strong network effects, operating leverage and new product opportunities (including the planned mineral exchange). Risks: trading volumes can reverse with volatility, SEBI position/contract rules can alter economics, technology outage risk, high valuation and concentration in precious-metals/energy products.
- **Action:** WAIT. The stock is at its 52-week high and P/E ranges from ~53x to >70x; wait for valuation normalisation rather than extrapolating a volatility-led quarter.
- Watch: commodity ADV, options mix, market share, transaction charges, new product contribution, technology resilience and regulatory changes.
- Sources: MCX official announcements/investor materials https://www.mcxindia.com/investor-relations/announcements-notices ; Q2 FY27 result analysis https://www.icicidirect.com/research/equity/rapid-results/multi-commodity-exchange-of-india-ltd ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/45693/multi-commodity-exchange-of-india-ltd/

## 5) Dr Lal PathLabs (LALPATHLAB) — HOLD / WAIT FOR A CORRECTION

## 7) United Breweries (UBL) — WAIT; earnings rebound, but returns and valuation remain weak
- Close ₹1,216; P/E approximately 66–78x; P/B about 7.1x; 52-week range ₹1,128.60–₹1,819.
- Q2 FY27 sell-in volumes +16%, premium volumes +38%, net sales +24%, EBITDA margin expanded 480 bps; consolidated PAT ₹135.3cr (+190.9%). However, ROE about 9.3%, ROCE 10.9% and debt/equity 0.26; annual cash flow and return ratios remain modest.
- Heineken brand, premiumisation and category recovery support growth. Risks include state excise/price regulation, distribution restrictions, competition, raw-material costs and valuation that is still ~66–78x despite the stock being well below its 52-week high.
- **Action:** WAIT. The quarter is strong, but a near-70x P/E is not justified by current return ratios; wait for sustainable margin and ROCE improvement.
- Watch: premium volume share, net sales realisation, EBITDA/hl, state policy, ROCE, debt and cash conversion.
- Sources: United Breweries Q2 FY27 release https://financialfilings.com/filings/united-breweries-ltd/interim-quarterly-report/2026/74798137/ ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/44989/united-breweries-ltd/

## 8) Britannia Industries (BRITANNIA) — ACCUMULATE ON CORRECTIONS
- Close ₹4,750.50; P/E approximately 42.9–45.2x; P/B approximately 22–27x; 52-week range ₹4,499.90–₹6,271.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated sales ₹5,542cr (+16.6%), volume growth 13%, net profit ₹726cr (+10.8%). Detailed filings show revenue from operations ₹5,597.8cr, EBITDA ₹1,095cr (+8.8%), margin 18.25% versus 19.72% a year ago.
- Strong brand, distribution, product breadth and volume-led growth support the long-term thesis. Risks include input inflation, margin compression, intense competition, premium valuation and food-claim/regulatory scrutiny; a CCPA penalty reported for a “100% whole wheat” advertising claim should be checked against the official order and remediation.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE ON CORRECTIONS in measured tranches; current price is close to its 52-week low, but ~43–45x earnings is not cheap and margins need to recover.
- Watch: volume growth, gross margin, ad spend, price/mix, wheat/dairy/sugar costs, market share and regulatory claims.

## 9) Zensar Technologies (ZENSARTECH) — WAIT FOR PORTFOLIO DIVERSIFICATION
- Close ₹549.55; P/E approximately 16x; P/B approximately 2.6x; dividend yield about 2.7%; 52-week range ₹416.15–₹758.90.
- Q2 FY27 revenue US$166.2m, +4.1% QoQ in constant currency and +11.5% YoY in INR; EBITDA margin 15.8% (+120 bps QoQ). Banking/financial services constant-currency growth +17.1% YoY; AI-led deals represented 36% of the order book.
- Lower valuation, net cash/low debt, dividend yield and improving margins make this more attractively priced than many IT peers. Risks include small scale, client concentration, US/Europe demand, deal conversion and limited growth visibility. The user's existing TCS holding is already about 60% of the Indian-stock portfolio, so another IT position is not the immediate priority.
- **Action:** WAIT FOR PORTFOLIO DIVERSIFICATION. Business valuation merits future review, but do not add IT exposure while TCS dominates the portfolio.
- Watch: constant-currency growth, AI-led order conversion, top-client concentration, EBITDA margin, attrition, cash flow and dividend sustainability.
- Sources: Zensar official Q2 FY27 release https://www.zensar.com/newsroom/press-releases/zensar-registered-41-sequential-constant-currency-revenue-growth-for-q2fy27 ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/45274/zensar-technologies-ltd/
