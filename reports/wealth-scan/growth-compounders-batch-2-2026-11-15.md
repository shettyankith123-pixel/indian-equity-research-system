## 1) Oracle Financial Services Software (OFSS) — ACCUMULATE ON CORRECTIONS
- Close ₹9,912; P/E approximately 24–33x depending on TTM EPS methodology; P/B about 11x; 52-week range ₹6,234.50–₹12,540.
- Q2 FY27 revenue ₹1,907cr (+7% YoY); net income ₹702cr (+29%). Business has high recurring product/support revenue, very high operating margin and ROE, negligible debt and a dividend yield around 4%.
- Sticky core banking/financial-services software and long client relationships support durability. Risks: customer concentration in global financial institutions, Oracle parent/technology ecosystem dependence, product transition and slower revenue growth than high-growth IT services peers.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE ON CORRECTIONS. Lower valuation than many Indian IT peers, strong margins and cash generation are attractive, but the 24–33x P/E spread shows EPS methodology matters; validate normalized earnings before a large position.
- Watch: license/support growth, cloud migration, large-bank client concentration, product renewals, cash conversion and parent-company strategy.
- Sources: Q2 FY27 results filing https://financialfilings.com/filings/oracle-financial-services-software-limited/earnings-release/2026/74661423/ ; market snapshot https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/oracle-financial-services-software-ltd/840

## 2) Coforge (COFORGE) — HOLD / WAIT FOR BETTER ENTRY
- Close ₹2,028; P/E approximately 45.6–57.8x; P/B about 4–7x depending on source; 52-week range ₹1,008.10–₹2,127.90.
- Q2 FY27 revenue ₹6,171cr (+53.6% YoY INR; +40.1% USD), EBITDA ₹1,270cr (+67.3%), EBIT margin 16.0% (+134 bps), PAT ₹588cr (+56.5%). Eight large deals were signed; next-12-month order book US$2.38bn (+45%); FY27 EBIT margin guidance raised to 16%.
- Exceptional growth and order visibility are strong positives. But the stock is near its 52-week high and P/E is high; acquisition integration (Cigniti/Encora), customer/vertical concentration, execution and margin sustainability matter.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT for a correction. Business momentum is excellent, but at ~46–58x earnings the price already discounts substantial execution. No chase.
- Watch: organic constant-currency growth, deal conversion, order-book quality, EBIT margin, acquisition integration, cash conversion and client concentration.
- Sources: Coforge official Q2 FY27 release https://news.coforge.com/newsroom/press-release/coforge-delivers-an-outstanding-performance-revenue-growth-536-yoy-raises-ebit-margin-guidance-for-the-year ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/43426/coforge-ltd/

## 3) Persistent Systems (PERSISTENT) — WAIT; acquisition and dilution risk
- Close ₹6,003; P/E approximately 53–55x; P/B approximately 7.7–12.1x; 52-week range ₹4,244.50–₹6,599.
- Q2 FY27 revenue US$487.6m (+20% YoY, +7.8% QoQ); INR revenue +30.3%; EBIT margin 15.0%; operating PAT +5.0%, while reported PAT fell 25.4% due to exceptional/acquisition-related impacts. The company secured about 94.04% of Nagarro share capital and approved an equity raise up to US$450m; combined equity and borrowings for the transaction capped at US$1.25bn.
- Strong growth and AI/engineering demand are positives. The proposed Nagarro transaction, QIP and debt funding introduce dilution, leverage, integration and margin risk; ICRA negative watch on NCDs requires careful review.
- **Action:** WAIT. Do not underwrite the acquisition as automatically value-accretive; wait for closing terms, financing mix, pro-forma leverage and synergy evidence. Valuation is demanding.
- Watch: organic growth, EBIT margin, net debt, acquisition integration, QIP dilution, ratings, client concentration and operating cash flow.
- Sources: Persistent official Q2 FY27 results https://www.persistent.com/media/press-releases/q2-fy27-results/ ; valuation https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/persistent-systems-ltd/434

## 4) Siemens (SIEMENS) — HOLD / WAIT
- Close ₹3,660; P/E approximately 39–40x; P/B approximately 8.3–9.4x; 52-week range ₹2,826–₹4,149.40.
- Q2 FY27 orders ₹6,228cr (+37.8%), revenue ₹5,734cr (+17%), order backlog ₹47,034cr (+12%), PAT ₹504cr (+4%). Profit from operations margin was 9.5%; management cited commodity and FX volatility affecting material costs.
- Strong infrastructure, mobility and industrial capex demand supports the order book. Counterpoints: modest profit growth relative to sales/orders, project mix, execution, commodity/FX exposure, and the premium valuation.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT. The order book is healthy, but the earnings conversion and margin trajectory need to catch up before paying ~40x earnings.
- Watch: order conversion, margin recovery, commodity/FX costs, working capital, project cancellations and structural impact of Siemens group restructuring.
- Sources: Siemens official Q2 FY27 release https://press.siemens.com/in/el/node/335 ; valuation https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/siemens-ltd/stocks/companyid-13102.cms

## 5) ABB India (ABB) — WAIT; strong orders, premium multiple
- Close ₹7,054.50; P/E approximately 50x (some providers report materially higher adjusted multiples); P/B about 16x; 52-week range ₹4,637.50–₹7,924.50.
- Q3 CY2026 orders ₹4,733cr (+56%), backlog ₹12,927cr (+36%), revenue ₹3,738cr (+19%), operational EBITA ₹484cr (+15%) and PAT ₹440cr (+10%). Operational EBITA margin 12.9% versus 13.4% a year earlier.
- Electrification, automation, data centres and industrial capex provide structural growth. However, margin contraction and a ~50x P/E mean expectations are high. Parent ABB's global results are not a substitute for ABB India standalone performance.
- **Action:** WAIT. Strong order intake is encouraging, but earnings conversion and margin recovery need to justify the premium; the analyst consensus mean target is roughly at the current price.
- Watch: order backlog conversion, margins, order quality, capacity investment, customer mix and working capital.
- Sources: ABB official Q3 CY2026 release https://www.abb.com/global/en/news/140418/expanding-demand-across-segments-solid-execution ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/40828/abb-india-ltd/

## 6) Cummins India (CUMMINSIND) — WAIT; margin reset before entry
- Close ₹4,369; P/E approximately 51–54x; P/B about 14.6x; 52-week range ₹3,883.10–₹6,100.
- Q2 FY27 standalone sales ₹3,247cr (+4% YoY); domestic sales ₹2,692cr (+5%); exports ₹555cr (+2%). PBT ₹601cr with an 18.5% margin, down 28.4% YoY; PAT ₹467cr. Management cited commodity and freight inflation plus product mix as margin pressures, with pricing actions expected to flow through progressively.
- Strong power-generation, data-centre, industrial and aftermarket franchise, healthy balance sheet and distribution reach are positives. But profit is falling while sales barely grow; valuation above 50x makes the margin decline material.
- **Action:** WAIT. Require evidence that price increases offset commodity/freight inflation and that margins recover before entry.
- Watch: domestic generator demand, data-centre orders, export mix, aftermarket growth, PBT margin, pricing pass-through and cash conversion.
- Sources: Cummins official Q2 FY27 press release https://www.cummins.com/sites/default/files/india/Legal/seintimationpressrelease_04112026.pdf ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/41706/cummins-india-ltd/

## 7) CG Power (CGPOWER) — WAIT; growth is real, valuation and regulatory risk are high

- Q2 FY27 revenue ₹3,802cr (+30–32% YoY), order intake +90%, order backlog ₹21,820cr, PAT +46% per company press release; consolidated PBT +44%.

INDmoney news feed reported a Maharashtra Pollution Control Board stop-work order for two units; this must be verified against the actual MPCB order, scope, remediation and current operating status before formal governance scoring.
- **Action:** WAIT. At roughly 95–108x earnings, the stock requires years of high growth; a regulatory disruption or subsidiary losses could materially affect valuation.
- Watch: order intake/backlog conversion, core power margins, semiconductor cash burn, capex, free cash flow and verified MPCB compliance status.
- Sources: CG Power Q2 FY27 press release https://financialfilings.com/filings/cg-power-and-industrial-solutions-limited/earnings-release/2026/74646964/ ; financial results https://financialfilings.com/filings/cg-power-and-industrial-solutions-limited/earnings-release/2026/74645840/ ; valuation https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/CGPOWER/financials/ratios/

## 8) Hindustan Aeronautics (HAL) — HOLD / WAIT FOR DELIVERY VISIBILITY
- Close ₹4,809.80; P/E approximately 33.6x; P/B approximately 7.3x; 52-week range ₹3,479.10–₹5,149.90.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹7,613cr (+14.9%), EBITDA approximately ₹1,836cr (+17%), PAT ₹1,936cr (+16%); operating margin around 22.8%, lower than the prior-year period. H1 consolidated PAT ₹3,525cr. Statutory auditors issued unmodified opinions.
- Strategic monopoly-like position in several Indian military aircraft/helicopter platforms, defence indigenisation and a long order pipeline support long-term demand. Counterpoints are delivery delays (including Tejas Mk1A engine/integration bottlenecks), provisional pricing, execution concentration, government procurement cycles and margin volatility.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT. Strong strategic position, but require evidence of aircraft delivery acceleration and margin normalisation before adding near the 52-week high.
- Watch: Tejas Mk1A deliveries, engine supply, helicopter execution, order book conversion, provisional pricing, working capital and audit qualifications (none reported in current results).
- Sources: HAL official audited Q2 FY27 results https://hal-india.co.in/backend/eechoogn/2026/08/IntegratedFinancial_30092026.pdf ; valuation https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/HAL/financials/ratios/
