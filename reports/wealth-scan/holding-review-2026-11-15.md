## Company assessments

### TCS — HOLD; no additional buying
Q2 FY27 revenue was US$7.642bn (+2.4% YoY in USD; +0.5% QoQ constant currency), operating margin 24.0%, net margin 19.0%, annualised AI revenue US$3.1bn and quarterly TCV US$9.6bn. Strong franchise and AI capability, but reported growth remains modest. INDmoney analyst consensus mean target was ₹2,375 versus ₹2,407.50 (40 analysts); this is not intrinsic value. TCS concentration is the dominant portfolio risk. Do not add; avoid an impulsive sale.

### Power Grid — ACCUMULATE selectively
Q2 FY27 consolidated PAT ₹3,866 crore (+8% YoY); H1 capex ₹16,864 crore; transmission availability 99.81%; six tariff-based transmission projects won in Q2. ₹257.45 was near the ₹242.30 52-week low. Regulated transmission franchise and pipeline are positives. Risks: capital intensity, leverage, interest costs, tariff/regulatory returns and commissioning/capitalisation. A 52-week low is not proof of undervaluation.

### SBI — ACCUMULATE in tranches
Q2 FY27 PAT +14.28% YoY to ₹23,038 crore; NII +14.69%; domestic NIM 3.11%; advances +17.84%; GNPA 1.39%, NNPA 0.36%, credit cost 0.22%. Trailing P/E reference was about 11.0x on 13 November. Improving asset quality and earnings support a positive view. Monitor the CASA ratio decline to 38.32% and funding-cost pressure. Gradual additions only, with review after results.

### ICICI Bank — HOLD; wait rather than chase
Q2 FY27 PAT +20.1% YoY to ₹14,843 crore; core operating profit +21.8%; loans +24.1% excluding FCNR(B); GNPA 1.20%, NNPA 0.30%. Trailing P/E reference about 19.3x. ₹1,583.60 was close to the ₹1,616 52-week high; INDmoney analyst mean target ₹1,746 (39 analysts). Strong execution, but do not chase near the high at a premium valuation.

### NMDC — HOLD / WAIT
Q2 FY27 standalone revenue ₹6,638 crore, EBITDA ₹2,434 crore, PAT ₹1,667 crore. Sequentially revenue fell 2.3%, EBITDA 13.6%, PAT 15.6%; production and sales also declined sequentially. At ₹69.80 versus 52-week range ₹65.15–₹97.49, it remains a cyclical iron-ore exposure. Prices, levies, production and sales dominate; lithium exploration is not a proven earnings driver. Do not add until operations and margins stabilise.

### Varun Beverages — HOLD / WAIT for margin evidence
Q3 CY2026 revenue +33.2% YoY, EBITDA +25.1%, PAT +13.5%; beverage volumes +28.7% to 354.4m cases, India +22.5%, international +39.9%. Profit growth lagged revenue growth, so cost/margin conversion needs monitoring. ₹432.80 was below the ₹555.80 52-week high and above ₹381 low. Strong volume growth alone does not justify adding without a valuation and margin review.

### Samvardhana Motherson — HOLD; do not chase
Q2 FY27 record revenue ₹38,280 crore (+27% YoY); H1 revenue ₹73,524 crore (+22%); Q2 EBITDA ₹3,353 crore; normalized PAT ₹1,175 crore; booked business US$106bn; leverage 0.8x. Strong order visibility, but global auto cyclicality, integration, capex and thin margins remain risks. At ₹164.80, close to ₹173.27 52-week high; INDmoney analyst mean target ₹173 (21 analysts). Hold the small position; do not chase.
