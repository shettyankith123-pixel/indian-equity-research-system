## Preliminary decision register

### 1) TCS — HOLD; do not add while portfolio weight is ~60%
**Business/quality thesis:** High-quality global IT services franchise with sticky enterprise relationships, strong cash conversion and a growing AI services opportunity. The business remains strategically relevant, but current revenue growth is subdued.
**Latest result:** Q2 FY27 revenue US$7.642bn, +2.4% YoY in USD and +0.5% QoQ in constant currency; operating margin 24.0%; net profit US$1.450bn; operating cash flow US$1.48bn, 102.2% of net income. AI annualised revenue reached US$3.1bn; Q2 TCV was US$9.6bn.
**Valuation context:** ₹2,407.50 close; trailing P/E about 16.9x (13-Nov market snapshot); 52-week range ₹1,976.80–₹3,350. INDmoney analyst consensus mean target ₹2,375, slightly below the close. This is not an intrinsic fair value.
**Risks/counter-evidence:** USD revenue growth remains low; AI monetisation must offset productivity-led pressure on traditional billable work; employee costs rose 10.1% YoY in H1 FY27 per INDmoney news feed; US Department of Labor PERM suspension report requires primary-source verification before treating as a confirmed material business restriction.
**Action:** HOLD existing 5 shares. No fresh purchase while TCS is nearly 60% of the portfolio. Reassess if revenue growth and deal conversion accelerate, or if concentration falls materially.
**Invalidation/watch items:** sustained loss of large clients, persistent margin compression, weaker cash conversion, or evidence that AI productivity reduces revenue faster than new AI work scales.
**Confidence:** Medium (strong official quarterly disclosure; governance/long-cycle valuation review still incomplete).
**Sources:** TCS Q2 FY27 release, 8-Oct-2026: https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-financial-results-q2-fy-2027 ; INDmoney price/valuation snapshot: https://www.indmoney.com/stocks/tata-consultancy-services-ltd-share-price

### 2) POWERGRID — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY; infrastructure/dividend diversifier
**Business/quality thesis:** Regulated national transmission utility with large installed network and long-duration capex pipeline. It diversifies the portfolio away from IT and consumer exposure.
**Latest result:** Q2 FY27 consolidated PAT ₹3,866cr, +8% YoY; total income ₹12,055cr, +3%; H1 capex ₹16,864cr; assets capitalised ₹9,300cr; transmission availability 99.81%. Won six transmission projects in Q2 under tariff-based competitive bidding.
**Valuation context:** ₹257.45 close; P/E about 14.8x, P/B about 2.4x, dividend yield about 3.5%; 52-week range ₹242.30–₹324.95. INDmoney analyst mean target ₹315 (+22.4%), which is sentiment context only.
**Risks/counter-evidence:** capital-intensive regulated business; execution and commissioning delays, leverage/refinancing costs, tariff/regulatory changes and capex-to-capitalisation slippage can affect returns. Q2 capex exceeded asset capitalisation.
**Action:** ACCUMULATE gradually in small tranches, not a lump-sum chase. Existing 10 shares are 12.8% of the equity portfolio; keep position sizing disciplined.
**Invalidation/watch items:** deterioration in transmission availability, material capex delays, regulatory return compression or leverage rising faster than commissioning.
**Confidence:** Medium-high on operating profile; medium on valuation pending long-term cash-flow model.
**Sources:** POWERGRID Q2 FY27 press release, 1-Nov-2026: https://powergrid.in/sites/default/files/press_document/Press%20release_Q2FY27%20-%20Final_01Nov2026.pdf ; market metrics: https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/power-grid-corporation-of-india-ltd/2172

**Valuation context:** ₹1,080 close; trailing P/E about 11.0x and P/B about 1.6x; 52-week range ₹933.90–₹1,234.70. INDmoney analyst mean target ₹1,270 (+17.6%), not intrinsic fair value.
**Risks/counter-evidence:** advances are growing faster than deposits, and CASA ratio has fallen; a sustained shift to higher-cost term deposits could pressure NIM. Public-sector governance/technology/cyber execution risks require ongoing monitoring.
**Action:** ACCUMULATE gradually; existing 2 shares are 10.7% of the portfolio. Prefer staggered adds rather than chasing a short-term rally.
**Invalidation/watch items:** CASA decline accelerates, deposit pricing drives NIM lower, credit costs rise, or asset quality reverses.
**Confidence:** Medium-high on current quarter; medium on long-run return outlook.
**Sources:** SBI Q2 FY27 results, 3-Nov-2026: https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17826/60584967/501.pdf/5af44658-bef3-752a-17ca-2f4175eaf555 ; analyst presentation: https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17836/1275616/SBI%2BAnalyst%2BPresentation%2BQ2FY27_03112026.pdf/f1632614-c85d-6bb0-d5dd-3a600e6b6b21?t=1793695579455 ; market metrics: https://m.economictimes.com/state-bank-of-india/stocks/companyid-11984.cms

### 4) ICICI Bank — HOLD; add only on a meaningful pullback
**Business/quality thesis:** Strong private-bank franchise with robust profitability, healthy capital and improving NPAs. The latest quarter is strong, but the share price is close to its 52-week high and valuation is not obviously cheap.
**Latest result:** Q2 FY27 PAT ₹14,843cr, +20.1% YoY; NII ₹25,339cr, +17.7%; core operating profit +21.8%; advances +30% headline (+24.1% excluding FCNR(B)-linked overseas branch balances); deposits +27.1% headline (+17.0% excluding FCNR(B)); NIM 4.18% vs 4.30% a year ago; GNPA 1.20%, NNPA 0.30%; CET1 16.01%.
**Valuation context:** ₹1,583.60 close; trailing P/E around 19.2x; 52-week range ₹1,187.60–₹1,616. INDmoney analyst mean target ₹1,746 (+10.3%), not intrinsic fair value.
**Risks/counter-evidence:** NIM compression, fast headline credit/deposit growth partly influenced by FCNR(B) flows, and high expectations embedded in valuation. Monitor whether domestic core loan/deposit growth sustains and whether credit costs remain contained.
**Action:** HOLD existing 1 share. Consider adding only after a meaningful pullback or when valuation offers a better margin of safety; do not treat analyst targets as a buy trigger.
**Invalidation/watch items:** NIM falls further without compensating fee/core operating growth, credit costs rise, or asset-quality deterioration appears.
**Confidence:** High on latest operating metrics; medium on entry valuation.
**Sources:** ICICI Bank Q2 FY27 performance review, 17-Oct-2026: https://www.icici.bank.in/about-us/news-room/2026/performance-review-quarter-ended-september-30-2026 ; quarterly reports index: https://www.icici.bank.in/about-us/qfr ; valuation snapshot: https://www.indmoney.com/stocks/icici-bank-ltd-share-price

**Latest result:** Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹6,638cr (+4.1% YoY); EBITDA ₹2,436cr (+2.4%); PAT about ₹1,667cr (down around 1.8% YoY); EBITDA margin about 36.7% vs 38.1% a year earlier. Q2 production 121.55 lakh tonnes; sales 104.83 lakh tonnes. Q2 production and sales were lower than Q1 sequentially.
**Valuation context:** ₹69.80 close; trailing P/E about 8.3x, P/B about 1.7x and dividend yield around 5%; 52-week range ₹65.15–₹97.49. Low P/E is not enough by itself to establish undervaluation in a cyclical commodity business. INDmoney analyst mean target ₹87, sentiment only.
**Risks/counter-evidence:** Iron ore prices/realisation, royalty and statutory levies, steel demand, volume execution, logistics and government ownership/policy. Recent profit and margin softness argues against automatic averaging.
**Action:** HOLD existing 15 shares; WAIT before adding until sales volumes, realisations and margins stabilise. Position is 5.2% of portfolio.
**Invalidation/watch items:** further margin contraction, sustained sales decline, adverse mining policy/levy changes, or weak cash conversion.
**Confidence:** Medium; commodity-cycle sensitivity is high.
**Sources:** NMDC Q2 FY27 investor presentation, 12-Nov-2026: https://nse-bse-announcements.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/nmdc/NMDC_12112026202204_NMDCSEInvestorPPTSep2026.pdf ; valuation snapshot: https://shop.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/43550/nmdc-ltd/

**Invalidation/watch items:** volume growth normalises sharply, margin pressure persists, debt/capex rises without returns, or PepsiCo distribution economics weaken.
**Confidence:** Medium-high on reported Q3 data; medium on long-term valuation.
