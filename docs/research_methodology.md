# Research methodology

## Investment policy defaults

- Default holding horizon: 7–10 years.
- Long-term investment research and swing trading are separate modes.
- Swing analysis is activated only by an explicit user request.
- Evaluate watchlist companies and include material outside-watchlist or IPO opportunities when evidence supports doing so.
- Use plain language and define technical terms.

## Company scan sections

1. Company and business model.
2. Latest quarterly and annual financial statements.
3. Five-to-ten-year trends where comparable data exists.
4. Industry structure, competitive position and peer set.
5. Profitability, cash conversion and capital allocation.
6. Sector-specific indicators:
   - Banks: deposits, advances, CASA, NIM, ROA, ROE, GNPA, NNPA, slippages, provisioning, PCR, CET1 and total capital.
   - Non-financials: growth, margins, cash flow, leverage, working capital, returns on capital and dilution.
7. Management, governance, related-party transactions and regulatory matters.
8. Valuation: relative and intrinsic methods appropriate to the sector.
9. Bull, base and bear cases.
10. Risks, monitoring indicators and thesis-invalidation conditions.
11. Final decision: BUY, ACCUMULATE, WAIT, OBSERVE or AVOID.

## Evidence rules

For every material claim, record:
- Source title and URL or provider reference.
- Source type: primary, regulator, exchange, broker, media or model-generated.
- Publication date and retrieval timestamp.
- Financial period and whether the figure is standalone or consolidated.
- Units and currency.
- Whether the figure is directly reported or calculated.
- Calculation formula and assumptions for derived figures.

Prioritise audited annual reports, official quarterly results, stock-exchange filings, regulatory notices and official investor presentations. Secondary sources may provide context but should not override primary filings.

## Valuation rules

- Use methods appropriate to the business model.
- Banks: justified price-to-book, residual income or dividend-based methods; show ROE, growth and cost-of-equity assumptions.
- Non-financials: select cash-flow, earnings, asset-based or sum-of-parts methods based on economics.
- Always show bear/base/bull assumptions, implied values, sensitivity and margin of safety.
- Never present a broker target as intrinsic value.

## Decision discipline

A BUY or ACCUMULATE decision requires business quality, financial strength, valuation and risk to be considered together. A WAIT rating may be appropriate for an excellent company at an unattractive price. State what evidence would change the rating.
