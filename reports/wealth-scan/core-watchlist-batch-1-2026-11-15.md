### HCL Technologies (HCLTECH) — ACCUMULATE ON DIPS
- Close ₹1,378; P/E approximately 20–22x; 52-week range ₹1,030–₹1,780.
- Q2 FY27 revenue ₹35,620cr, +11.5% YoY in INR and +2.6% YoY constant currency; EBIT margin 18.2%; net income ₹5,132cr (+21.2%); new deal TCV US$3.258bn; advanced AI revenue US$219m in-quarter (+78% YoY CC); LTM FCF/net income 87%; ROIC 41.2%.
- FY27 CC revenue guidance 2.5–4.0% is cautious; the company cites volatile demand. AI growth is encouraging but is not yet large enough to erase traditional IT demand and pricing risks. News of US PERM application suspension requires primary-source legal verification before treating it as a material operating restriction.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE ON DIPS in measured tranches; do not chase. High cash conversion, ROIC and AI momentum support long-term quality, but low guidance and volatile client spending cap conviction.
- Watch: constant-currency growth, EBIT margin, deal conversion, AI revenue contribution, FCF conversion and any verified immigration/labour restriction.
- Analyst mean target ₹1,267.50 is below market; this reinforces entry discipline, not a sell signal.

### Polycab India (POLYCAB) — ACCUMULATE ON CORRECTIONS
- Close ₹7,904; P/E approximately 37.8x; P/B about 9.1x; 52-week range ₹6,663–₹10,126.
- Q2 FY27 revenue ₹9,899cr (+52.8% YoY), EBITDA ₹1,511cr (+41.7%) and PAT ₹977cr (+42.6%). H1 revenue +46%, EBITDA +36%, PAT +38%. Net cash ₹45.2bn at 30-Sep-2026; working-capital cycle approximately 11 days.
- Growth is strong and supported by wires/cables demand, electrification, construction and FMEG expansion. However, quarterly operating margin remained below the prior-year quarter despite sequential improvement; valuation is demanding. A disputed insolvency proceeding was reportedly set aside by NCLAT; check the final court order and any residual litigation before governance clearance.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE ON CORRECTIONS, not at any price. Strong growth and net cash justify continued research priority, but ~38x earnings and execution expectations leave limited room for disappointment.
- Watch: margin recovery, working-capital quality, FMEG profitability, EHV capacity commissioning, promoter pledging (reported 0%) and litigation closure.
- Analyst mean target ₹10,150 is market sentiment, not intrinsic value.
- Sources: Q2 FY27 results release https://financialfilings.com/filings/polycab-india-limited/earnings-release/2026/72594788/ ; earnings call transcript https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/POLYCAB_19102026163912_Intimation_of_Earnings_Call_Transcript.pdf ; market snapshot https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/249951/polycab-india-ltd/

### UltraTech Cement (ULTRACEMCO) — HOLD / WAIT FOR BETTER VALUE
- Close ₹12,100; P/E approximately 40.4x; P/B approximately 4.8x; 52-week range ₹10,325–₹13,110.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated net sales ₹23,024cr (+18.9% YoY), PBIDT ₹3,832cr (+17.3%) and PAT ₹1,501cr (+21.3%). Domestic volumes grew 13.8%. Domestic capacity reported at 204.7 MTPA; green energy capacity exceeded 2 GW and met about 48% of power needs.
- Scale, brand, distribution and capacity position are strong. Risks are cement pricing discipline, fuel/power/freight costs, regional competition, capacity utilisation and integration/returns from capacity expansion. Current valuation already discounts a strong earnings trajectory.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT. Do not chase a ~40x P/E for a cyclical cement producer; reassess if valuation compresses or earnings growth sustainably outpaces capacity additions.
- Watch: cement realisations, EBITDA/tonne, fuel costs, utilisation, net debt and returns on new capacity.
- Analyst mean target ₹13,930 is sentiment context only.
- Sources: company Q2 FY27 release https://www.ultratechcement.com/corporate/media/press-releases/financial-results-q2fy27 ; valuation snapshot https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/44964/ultratech-cement-ltd/

### Pidilite Industries (PIDILITIND) — WAIT
- Close ₹1,525.70; P/E approximately 58x; P/B approximately 13.9x; 52-week range ₹1,259–₹1,707.50.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated sales ₹4,285cr (+21.1% YoY), EBITDA ₹878cr (+3.1%), PAT ₹626cr (+7.1%); EBITDA margin 20.5% versus 24.0% a year earlier. Standalone underlying volume growth was 10.7%.
- Fevicol/Dr Fixit and distribution create a durable brand moat, and volume growth is healthy. But input-cost inflation has compressed margins, and valuation remains exceptionally high relative to current profit growth. Price increases and mix need to restore margin without harming volumes.
- **Action:** WAIT. Quality is not the same as value; do not buy solely because the franchise is excellent at ~58x earnings.
- Watch: gross margin recovery, EBITDA margin, price/mix elasticity, volume growth, B2B/consumer split and raw-material prices.
- Analyst mean target ₹1,800 is not intrinsic fair value.
- Sources: Pidilite Q2 FY27 release https://www.pidilite.com/content/dam/pidilitecorporatewebsite/listing-information/stock-exchange-announcements/intimation-and-updates/fy-2026-27/se-intimation-press-release-30th-september-2026.pdf ; investor relations https://www.pidilite.com/investor-relations/listing-information ; valuation snapshot https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/43781/pidilite-industries-ltd/

### Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M) — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY
- Close ₹3,333; P/E approximately 19–21x; P/B approximately 4.2–4.8x; 52-week range ₹2,744.30–₹3,839.90.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹57,553cr (+25%) and PAT ₹4,588cr (+25%); annualised ROE 21%. Auto volumes +26%, SUV revenue market share 24.3%; consolidated Auto PAT +55%. Farm consolidated revenue fell 6% and PAT fell 9%; tractor volumes declined 6%. Services revenue +18%.
- Strong SUV/LCV franchise, EV optionality and diversified businesses are positives. Farm weakness, commodity costs, auto-cycle exposure, group complexity and capital allocation across subsidiaries are counterweights.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY on market weakness; avoid a concentrated one-shot entry. Valuation near 19–21x looks more reasonable than many premium consumer names, but earnings mix must be monitored.
- Watch: SUV market share, EV unit economics, farm recovery, standalone auto margins, group ROE and subsidiary cash requirements.
- Analyst mean target ₹4,200 is sentiment only.
- Sources: M&M Q2 FY27 results https://www.mahindra.com/news-room/press-release/en/m-and-m-results-q2-F27 ; valuation snapshot https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/43180/mahindra-mahindra-ltd/

### Maruti Suzuki (MARUTI) — WAIT; strong volumes, weak operating leverage
- Close ₹12,854; P/E approximately 30x; P/B approximately 3.7x; 52-week range ₹10,985–₹17,370.
- Q2 FY27 sales volume reached a record 696,654 units (+26.5% YoY); domestic market share rose 2.2 percentage points to 40.8%; net sales reached ₹50,615.5cr (+26.1%). EBIT fell to ₹2,803.7cr from ₹3,382cr and PAT fell to ₹2,414.9cr from ₹3,302.8cr, due to commodity cost pressure and lower non-operating income/mark-to-market losses. Pending bookings and low inventory indicate demand strength.
- Strong brand, distribution, scale and product pipeline are major positives. But volume growth has not translated into profit growth; export volumes were slightly lower and margin recovery depends on input costs and pricing.
- **Action:** WAIT. Do not interpret record sales as automatically bullish for earnings. Revisit after EBIT margin recovery or a better valuation entry.
- Watch: commodity costs, discounts, domestic share, export volumes, EV/hybrid product economics, inventory and EBIT/vehicle.
- Analyst mean target ₹15,802 is sentiment context only.
- Sources: company Q2/H1 FY27 results https://www.marutisuzuki.com/corporate/media/press-releases/2026/october/maruti-suzuki-india-limited-financial-results-q2-and-h1-fy-2026-27 ; valuation snapshot https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/MARUTI/financials/ratios/

### Apollo Hospitals (APOLLOHOSP) — HOLD / WAIT
- Close ₹8,526; P/E approximately 54.7x; P/B approximately 11.6x; 52-week range ₹6,696.50–₹9,070.50.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹7,615cr (+23% YoY), EBITDA ₹1,191cr (+29%) and PAT ₹630cr (+32%). Hospital occupancy rose to 73% from 69%; healthcare services revenue +23%. Expansion plan targets about 5,800 additional beds over five years; new hospitals are still ramping and reported EBITDA losses.
- High-quality healthcare platform with strong specialty mix, medical tourism and pharmacy ecosystem. But capacity expansion is capital-intensive, new beds need time to mature, and ~55x earnings leaves a high execution bar.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT. Strong business, but wait for a better margin of safety or evidence that new-bed returns are meeting plan.
- Watch: occupancy, ARPOB, mature vs new hospital margins, capex/debt, HealthCo profitability and regulatory developments.
- Analyst mean target ₹10,000 is sentiment, not fair value.

- Watch: specialty sales, US generics, regulatory inspections, litigation, Organon integration, leverage/net cash and adjusted-to-reported earnings reconciliation.
- Analyst mean target ₹2,180 is sentiment only.
