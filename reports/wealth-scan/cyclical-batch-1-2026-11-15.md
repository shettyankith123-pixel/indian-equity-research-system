## 1) Dalmia Bharat (DALBHARAT) — WAIT; cement cycle and earnings quality
- Close ₹1,491.90; P/E snapshots diverge materially (about 24.6x to 56.9x); P/B about 1.56x; 52-week range ₹1,319–₹2,260.
- Q2 FY27 revenue ₹3,563cr (+4% YoY), EBITDA ₹493cr, impacted by input-cost headwinds; consolidated PAT reported a loss of about ₹205cr. Sales volume was 7.1mt (+3%); renewable power share rose to 52%. Belgaum 3.6mt clinker line trial run began in October.
- Expansion and green power are positives, but weak pricing/cost absorption and the quarterly loss show that volume alone is not enough. Cement realisations, fuel/power costs, freight and regional competition are key.
- **Action:** WAIT. P/E is not a reliable single metric while quarterly earnings are distorted; do not average down until margins and profitability recover.
- Watch: EBITDA/tonne, trade/non-trade pricing, fuel costs, clinker utilisation, net debt and new capacity ramp-up.
- Sources: Dalmia Bharat Q2 FY27 release https://financialfilings.com/filings/dalmia-bharat-limited/earnings-release/2026/74753594/ ; valuation snapshot https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/dalmia-bharat-ltd/848

## 2) SRF (SRF) — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY ON WEAKNESS
- Close ₹2,475; P/E approximately 32–40x; P/B approximately 4.8–5.2x; 52-week range ₹2,178.30–₹3,143.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹4,904cr (+35%), operational EBIT ₹816cr (+26%) and PAT ₹509cr (+31%). Business mix includes specialty chemicals/fluorochemicals, packaging films and technical textiles.
- Recovery across chemicals and diversified end markets supports growth. Risks include global refrigerant/fluorochemical regulation, chemical pricing, input costs, capex intensity and leverage; 5-year return has been modest despite the recent quarter.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY on weakness, not aggressively. Q2 recovery is encouraging, but margins and capital returns must be sustained across the cycle.
- Watch: specialty-chemical margins, refrigerant phase-down rules, packaging-film spreads, debt/EBITDA, capex returns and operating cash flow.
- Sources: SRF official Q2 FY27 release https://www.srf.com/news/srf-limited-reports-strong-growth-in-q2-h1-fy27 ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/44172/srf-ltd/

## 3) Navin Fluorine (NAVINFLUOR) — WAIT; strong growth already priced in
- Close ₹10,002; P/E approximately 56.5–77.4x; P/B approximately 11.5–12.9x; 52-week range ₹5,440–₹10,165.
- H1 FY27 revenue grew 50.7% and EBITDA 68.8%; Q2 PAT increased 81.8% YoY; H1 revenue ₹2,286.5cr. New HFC R32 plant at Surat commenced operations.
- Strong specialty fluorochemicals, high-value fluoropolymers and CDMO opportunities support long-term growth. But price is close to the 52-week high and the P/E is very high; analyst mean target ₹8,511 is below the close. Growth execution is already heavily reflected in valuation.
- **Action:** WAIT. Do not chase the current momentum; wait for a price reset or earnings growth to catch up with valuation.
- Watch: new plant utilisation, CDMO order book, fluoropolymer qualification, margins, working capital and capex returns.
- Sources: Q2/H1 FY27 investor materials https://www.nfil.in/investor-relations/ ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/43484/navin-fluorine-international-ltd/

## 4) Gujarat Fluorochemicals (FLUOROCHEM) — WAIT; growth versus weak returns and promoter pledge
- Close ₹4,712.10; P/E approximately 76–90x; P/B approximately 6.2–6.6x; 52-week range ₹2,916.60–₹4,958.90.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹1,760cr (+45%), EBITDA ₹473cr (+32%) and PAT ₹240cr (+42%). Chemical segment revenue ₹1,723cr (+42%) and PAT ₹290cr (+46%). Battery materials continued to record losses; H1 working-capital days improved by 40 to 152.
- Fluoropolymers, fluorochemicals and battery materials provide structural growth opportunities. But ROE/ROCE remain low (about 7.6%/10%), working capital is still high and valuation is elevated. Market data reports promoter pledge of about 7.5%; this must be reconciled against the latest shareholding filing and pledge disclosures before any governance pass.
- **Action:** WAIT. Strong revenue growth does not yet justify a 76–90x multiple when capital returns are low and battery materials remain loss-making.
- Watch: promoter pledge status, ROCE, working-capital days, battery-materials losses, fluoropolymer capacity utilisation and free cash flow.
- Sources: GFL Q2 FY27 highlights https://gfl.co.in/upload/pages/188c7d47105a07ff0bf7fda54e1fa0f4.pdf ; earnings call https://gfl.co.in/upload/pages/40c7a7644d8c7d9ba5f776898d1eb2fc.pdf ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/258117/gujarat-fluorochemicals-ltd/

## 5) Deepak Nitrite (DEEPAKNTR) — HOLD / ACCUMULATE ONLY AFTER CASH-FLOW CONFIRMATION
- Close ₹1,594.40; P/E approximately 39.5x; P/B about 3.7x; 52-week range ₹1,280–₹1,897.90.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹3,076cr (+61.8%), PAT ₹398cr; PBT ₹543cr. H1 revenue ₹5,682cr (+48%), EBITDA ₹1,197cr (+173%), margin approximately 21%, PAT ₹743cr (+221%). Phenolics revenue was ₹2,291cr, +72% YoY, with EBIT margin around 22%.
- Recovery in phenolics and better utilisation are positives. Risks include commodity/feedstock cycle, phenol/acetone spreads, customer concentration, environmental/safety compliance, and working-capital/capex requirements. ROE/ROCE remain modest relative to the headline rebound.
- **Action:** HOLD / ACCUMULATE ONLY AFTER CASH-FLOW CONFIRMATION. Earnings have rebounded sharply from a weak base; wait for two or more quarters of durable margin and cash conversion before treating it as a new compounder.
- Watch: phenolics spread, specialty segment mix, operating cash flow, working capital, plant utilisation and environmental/safety disclosures.
- Sources: Deepak Nitrite Q2/H1 FY27 call transcript https://www.godeepak.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Deepak-Nitrite-Q2-H1-FY27-Concall-Transcript.pdf ; valuation https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/deepak-nitrite-ltd/1867

## 6) PI Industries (PIIND) — WAIT; export downturn is material
- Close ₹1,714.80; P/E approximately 19.7x (TTM multiple may lag the latest earnings collapse); P/B about 2.3x; 52-week range ₹1,699.40–₹3,819.90.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹1,702cr (-9%), EBITDA ₹236cr (-57%) and PAT ₹122cr (-70%). Agchem exports fell 18% due to weak demand, genericisation and pricing pressure; domestic business grew 19%; biologicals grew strongly (global +250%, domestic +45%); trade working-capital days reduced by 18.
- Strong R&D, molecule discovery and domestic biologicals are positives. But export weakness and severe profit decline are material; the low-looking P/E is based on trailing earnings that may not reflect the new run-rate.
- **Action:** WAIT. Do not assume the earnings trough is behind the company until export order flow and margins stabilise.
- Watch: export order book, new molecule launches, biologicals contribution, EBITDA margin, customer inventory, working capital and cash conversion.
- Sources: PI Industries official Q2 FY27 presentation https://www.piindustries.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Performance-of-the-Company-for-the-quarter-ended-September-30-2026.pdf ; valuation https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/pi-industries-ltd/1405

## 7) Hindalco (HINDALCO) — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY; strong earnings, higher leverage
- Close ₹943.60; P/E approximately 11.1–11.3x; P/B approximately 1.3x; 52-week range ₹771.85–₹1,176.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹91,708cr (+39%), EBITDA ₹13,757cr (+42%) and PAT ₹7,533cr (+59%). Aluminium upstream EBITDA ₹7,009cr (+55%); Novelis adjusted EBITDA ₹4,858cr (+32%); copper EBITDA ₹1,673cr (+164%). Net debt/EBITDA increased to 1.80x from 1.23x a year ago.
- Integrated aluminium/copper business and Novelis recovery support earnings. Risks: LME price cycle, energy/coal costs, Novelis execution and Bay Minette commissioning, higher leverage, global trade policy and large capex.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY in small tranches; valuation is reasonable relative to current earnings, but do not extrapolate commodity peak profits and monitor debt.
- Watch: aluminium realisations, Novelis shipments/EBITDA per tonne, debt/EBITDA, Bay Minette ramp-up, coal supply and free cash flow.
- Sources: Hindalco official Q2 FY27 release https://www.hindalco.com/media/press-releases/hindalco-results-q2fy27 ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/42415/hindalco-industries-ltd/

## 8) JSW Steel (JSWSTEEL) — HOLD / WAIT; cyclical recovery is priced in
- Close ₹1,348; P/E approximately 11.6–14.8x; P/B approximately 2.9–3.3x; 52-week range ₹1,073.20–₹1,370.
- Q2 FY27 India operations production 7.07mt (+5% YoY), sales 7.27mt (+14%), revenue ₹50,188cr (+30%), adjusted EBITDA ₹9,636cr (+40%) and EBITDA/tonne ₹13,248. Consolidated net profit attributable to owners was ₹5,279cr, +225% YoY, on a pro-forma comparable basis; reported consolidated revenue ₹54,486cr (+20.7%).
- Scale, capacity and strong volume/realisation recovery are positives. Risks include global steel cycle, Chinese exports, coking-coal/iron-ore costs, high capex, debt (debt/equity about 0.95) and overseas asset restructuring. Profit growth is off a weak base and margins can reverse quickly.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT. Do not chase near the 52-week high; buy only when the steel-cycle outlook and valuation offer a clear margin of safety.
- Watch: EBITDA/tonne, spreads, coking coal, domestic prices, net debt/EBITDA, capacity expansion and overseas asset sales.
- Sources: JSW Steel Q2 FY27 report https://financialfilings.com/filings/jsw-steel-limited/interim-quarterly-report/2026/74646829/ ; valuation https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/jsw-steel-ltd/1124
