## 1) Tata Steel (TATASTEEL) — HOLD / WAIT
- Close ₹186.68; P/E approximately 20–22x; P/B about 2.3x; 52-week range ₹160.06–₹224.40.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹65,436cr, EBITDA ₹10,665cr (+17% YoY); EBITDA margin about 16%; reported PAT ₹3,700cr versus ₹3,183cr. India business EBITDA ₹10,634cr on ₹42,653cr revenue (25% margin). UK business reported EBITDA loss of £56m. Net debt ₹91,423cr; net debt/EBITDA 2.4x.
- India business is strong and domestic capacity expansion/Neelachal integration supports long-term scale. Counterweights are UK restructuring losses, high net debt/capex and global steel-price cyclicality.
- **Action:** HOLD / WAIT. India operating strength is real, but consolidated returns remain burdened by UK losses and 2.4x net debt/EBITDA. Do not buy solely on a low-looking cyclical P/E.
- Watch: UK cash burn/restructuring, India EBITDA/tonne, net debt, capex, NINL integration and European energy/carbon costs.

## 2) Coal India (COALINDIA) — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY; dividend and low valuation
- Close ₹403.50; P/E approximately 7.5–8x; P/B about 2.1x; dividend yield around 6.6%; 52-week range ₹369.60–₹491.25.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated PAT ₹6,516cr (+53% YoY); revenue from operations +21%. H1 coal production 321mt versus 329.1mt a year earlier (down), while offtake rose 7.6% to 384.2mt. October production reached 67.2mt and supplies 70.3mt; H1 capex ₹7,966cr (+27%).
- Dominant domestic coal supplier with strong cash generation, dividend support and power-sector demand. Risks: long-term energy transition, lower production, labour/land logistics, government price controls, capex and diversification execution.
- **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY in small tranches. Low P/E and dividend yield provide support, but do not treat coal as a perpetual-growth business.
- Watch: production versus offtake, e-auction premium, grade slippage, capex productivity, subsidiary IPO plans and renewable diversification.
- Sources: Q2 results summary https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/earnings/coal-india-q2-results-net-profit-rises-57-to-rs-6-547-crore-announces-dividend-14063703.html ; market metrics https://www.etmoney.com/stocks/coal-india-ltd/1527

## 3) Hindustan Zinc (HINDZINC) — HOLD / ACCUMULATE ONLY ON CORRECTIONS
- Close ₹554.85; P/E approximately 11.2–17x depending on EPS provider; P/B approximately 7.8–10.4x; 52-week range ₹453.50–₹733.
- Q2 FY27 revenue ₹15,093cr (+77%), EBITDA ₹9,285cr (+108%), PAT ₹6,412cr (+142%); EBITDA margin 62%. Mined metal production 271kt (+5%), refined metal 264kt (+7%), silver production 173 tonnes (+20%). Free cash flow before growth capex ₹6,389cr.
- Low-cost integrated zinc/lead/silver operations and strong cash generation are positives. But current profits are highly sensitive to zinc/silver prices; P/B is high, and parent Vedanta's capital allocation, related-party, debt and dividend decisions require close governance monitoring.
- **Action:** HOLD existing watchlist status; ACCUMULATE ONLY ON CORRECTIONS after commodity-price normalisation and parent-level governance review. Avoid extrapolating record margins indefinitely.
- Watch: zinc/silver prices, cost of production, production guidance, dividends versus growth capex, promoter pledge/encumbrance, related-party transactions and parent leverage.
- Sources: Hindustan Zinc official Q2 FY27 release https://www.hzlindia.com/news-and-media/press-release/hindustan-zinc-2-q-net-profit-rises-142-yo-y-to-6-412-crore-highest-ever-quarterly-ebitda-driven-by-record-production-and-lower-zinc-cost-of-production ; valuation https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/42437/hindustan-zinc-ltd/

## 4) BHEL (BHEL) — WAIT; strong order book, valuation too high
- Close ₹436.30; P/E approximately 53–95x depending on adjusted/TTM EPS; P/B about 5.8x; 52-week range ₹239.17–₹464.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹9,300cr (+23.8%), EBITDA approximately ₹1,044cr (+75%), PAT ₹821cr (+119%). Order book ₹2.58 lakh crore (+17.3% YoY); H1 orders secured around ₹30,000cr; working capital improved to 141 days and customer collections were strong.
- Improved execution and thermal/nuclear/transmission/defence orders support a turnaround. But ROE around 6%, ROCE around 8%, historic execution delays and valuation above 50x (or much higher on some EPS bases) make the risk/reward unattractive at the current price.
- **Action:** WAIT. Revisit after more quarters of cash conversion and sustained ROE improvement; order book alone does not guarantee shareholder returns.
- Watch: order conversion, project delays/claims, working capital, customer collections, gross margin, cash flow and return on capital.

## 7) ONGC (ONGC) — ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY; cheap, but production and oil-price risks
- Close ₹225; P/E approximately 5.7–6.8x; P/B about 0.7–0.8x; dividend yield around 5.7–5.9%; 52-week range ₹218.53–₹307.50.
- Q2 FY27 standalone gross revenue ₹43,875cr (+32.8%), standalone PAT ₹15,489cr (+57.3%), consolidated PAT ₹18,535cr (+46.9%). Declared interim dividend ₹8/share and awarded two Western Offshore development projects worth ₹11,800cr; two new hydrocarbon discoveries were notified.
- Low valuation, dividend and upstream resource base provide potential value. Risks include crude/gas prices, production declines, government windfall taxes/price controls, exploration execution, overseas receivables and environmental incidents. - **Action:** ACCUMULATE GRADUALLY only as a cyclical/value allocation, not as a core compounder. Keep position sizing moderate and do not extrapolate a high-profit quarter.
- Watch: crude realisation, production volumes, new-well ramp-up, capex, overseas receivables, environmental compliance and dividend coverage.
